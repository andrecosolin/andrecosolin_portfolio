About

QA that understands the product, not just the bug.

Hi, I am André Cosolin, a Senior QA Engineer based in Araraquara, Brazil. My main focus is manual and exploratory testing for digital payments, e-commerce, Android apps and APIs. I work remotely with teams in Brazil and abroad, with a clear scope and deliverables agreed from the start.

I started at Newcon Software with manual functional testing and test cycle organization in Jira. I later spent more than two years at KaBuM!, testing high-traffic e-commerce flows, including its Build Your PC configurator. I also worked on a Playwright automation proof of concept, performance testing with Locust and data checks using SQL.

After that, I worked through Sensedia on a SmartPOS project for Getnet. I am now a Senior QA Engineer at Getnet, working with payment apps across 7 to 8 Android terminal models used in Brazil, Chile and Argentina. I also validate REST APIs with Postman and SQL, and prepare test plans, scenarios and clear bug reports that help teams reproduce issues. At Getnet, I helped organize the test repository and standardize test cycles and metrics. During that period, the domain's Quality Score rose from 35% to 76% in one quarter.

I focus on understanding expected behavior, exploring user flows and documenting findings in a useful way. I also use AI to help create automated tests. Choosing scenarios, reviewing code and validating results remain my responsibility.

Before moving into technology, I spent more than seven years coordinating logistics and industrial operations at companies including Embraer, CPFL Energia and Cutrale. That experience shaped the way I look at end to end processes, operational bottlenecks and the impact of software issues on customers.

I am pursuing a postgraduate program in Software Quality Engineering and Testing at PUC Minas. I also hold an MBA in Project Management and a degree in Information Security from Fatec.
